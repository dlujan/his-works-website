Deleting your account

Instructions for deleting your account:

  1. Log into the HisWorks app
  2. Navigate to the right-most tab (Account)
  3. Select "Account" at the top of the list to open Account Details
  4. Press the red Delete Account button and confirm

All data associated with your account will be immediately deleted and unretrievable.
